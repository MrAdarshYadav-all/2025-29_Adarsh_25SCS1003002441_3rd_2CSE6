# Artificial Intelligence Internship

A collection of learning activities, practical tasks, and project work completed during my **Artificial Intelligence Internship at Codec Technologies Pvt. Ltd.**

The internship was undertaken to gain practical exposure to Artificial Intelligence and understand how theoretical concepts learned during academic studies can be applied in practical and industry-oriented environments.

The internship focused on developing technical understanding through training, assigned tasks, project work, and practical learning activities.
