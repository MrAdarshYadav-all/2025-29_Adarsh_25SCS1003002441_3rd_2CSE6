1. INTRODUCTION

The TCS iON Career Edge – Young Professional programme is a professional
development course designed to prepare students for workplace success.
It focuses on communication, presentation, interview preparation,
professional etiquette, problem-solving, IT foundational skills and an
overview of Artificial Intelligence.

The programme was completed from 11 August 2026 to 25 August 2026 and
provides professional skills that complement the technical knowledge
gained in B.Tech CSE.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. ORGANIZATION PROFILE

TCS iON is a strategic business unit of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS).
It provides technology-enabled learning, assessment and skill
development solutions for students, educational institutions and
professionals.

The Career Edge – Young Professional course is designed to improve
workplace readiness and career preparation for students entering
professional environments.
